✨ PEMDAS Cheat Sheet ✨

Letter Meaning Example
P Parentheses (2 + 3) × 4 = 20
E Exponents 2³ = 8
M Multiplication 3 × 4 = 12
D Division 12 ÷ 3 = 4
A Addition 5 + 2 = 7
S Subtraction 9 − 4 = 5

🔑 Rules to Remember

  • Always solve inside parentheses first.
  • Apply exponents after parentheses.
  • Do multiplication and division from left to right.
  • Do addition and subtraction from left to right.

🧮 Example Problem

Solve: 8 + (3 × 2)² ÷ 6

  1. Parentheses: 3 × 2 = 6 → 8 + 6² ÷ 6
  2. Exponents: 6² = 36 → 8 + 36 ÷ 6
  3. Division: 36 ÷ 6 = 6 → 8 + 6
  4. Addition: 8 + 6 = 14
